# Corpus licence notes

## NPTEL (7 of 9 videos)

Source of truth: `https://onlinecourses-archive.nptel.ac.in/modules/nptel/assets/html/terms.html`,
fetched and saved verbatim as `corpus/nptel_terms_of_use_raw.html` (raw HTML, not paraphrased).

Exact quoted clause, "COPYRIGHT" section:

> All content or other materials available on the Sites, including but not limited to code,
> images, text, layouts, arrangements, displays, illustrations, audio and video clips, HTML
> files and other content are the property of NPTEL and/or its affiliates or licensors and are
> protected by copyright. All rights in the Site and its content, if not expressly granted, are
> reserved by NPTEL.

No mention of Creative Commons, CC BY-SA, or any open licence grant anywhere in this document
(checked: the word "creative" does not appear in the page at all). This resolves the disputed
2012/2014 CC BY-SA claim, at least as far as NPTEL's own current site terms state it: NPTEL
asserts full copyright, all rights reserved, not a CC licence. The 2012/2014 CC BY-SA claims
found in older secondary sources are not reflected in the current terms page and should not be
relied on.

**Basis for use in this project.** Academic, non-commercial, course-project and research use
(BITE314L coursework, non-commercial arXiv preprint), with source videos and derived tiers not
publicly redistributed beyond that context. This is not a licence grant from NPTEL, it is this
project relying on educational/research fair-use norms given no open licence is offered. If this
project's use ever extends beyond that (public redistribution, commercial use), this needs
separate legal review before proceeding — do not assume the above basis extends automatically.

## Non-commercial flag

Every source on this corpus is NC (non-commercial) at best: NPTEL grants no licence at all
(fallback to research/fair-use norms, inherently non-commercial), MIT OCW is explicitly
NC-restricted. **None of this corpus may be used toward anything commercial** without separate
licensing. Fine for the BITE314L course project and a non-commercial arXiv preprint; flag this
now so it is not a surprise later if scope changes.
